🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

If K3s won't start:
  → pkill -f "k3s server"
  → ./scripts/start-everything.sh

If pods won't start:
  → kubectl describe pod <name> -n smart-city
  → kubectl logs <name> -n smart-city

If service not accessible:
  → kubectl port-forward -n smart-city svc/<service> 8000:80 &
  → curl http://localhost:8000/health

More help in README.md troubleshooting section!
